Aquanexus — Water for AI

A Climate-Resilient Framework for Energy-Efficient Data Center Cooling

A water-energy-climate decision engine for AI data centres in water-stressed MENA countries, built for the UNESCO–Khalifa University Global Water Hackathon ahead of the UN Global Water Conference in Abu Dhabi (December 2026).

  • Enumerates every technology, water and energy combination for data-centre cooling.
  • Finds the Pareto-optimal frontier across five objectives: water, energy, cost, carbon and our Water-Energy Resilience Index.
  • Generates a plain-language recommendation explaining the best configuration to choose.
  • I formed and lead a team of eight across disciplines and countries: five colleagues, two academic advisors and one industrial advisor.
