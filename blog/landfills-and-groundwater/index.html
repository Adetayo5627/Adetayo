What happens to groundwater near a landfill?

05 October 2026 · 1 min read

groundwaterresearchwater quality

For my undergraduate thesis at the University of Ibadan, I studied how the Awotan dumpsite affects groundwater quality in the surrounding communities of Awotan and Apete.

Why this matters

Many households in Nigerian cities depend on private hand-dug wells and boreholes. When waste is dumped in unlined sites, rainwater can carry contaminants (leachate) down into the aquifer that feeds those wells.

How I approached it

  1. Sampled water from private wells at different distances from the dumpsite.
  2. Analysed physical, chemical and biological parameters using spectrophotometers, chromatographs and pH meters.
  3. Used statistical analysis in Python and Power BI to test how pollutant levels related to distance from the site.

Read the full paper

The study was published in Science, Engineering and Technology: doi.org/10.54327/set2025/v5.i1.161.
