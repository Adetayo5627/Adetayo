Welcome to my website

10 October 2026 · 1 min read

personalwater

Hello, and welcome! I’m Ibrahim Adetayo Lawal, a water resources engineer currently studying for an MSc in Water Engineering at the Pan African University Institute for Water and Energy Sciences (PAUWES) in Tlemcen, Algeria.

I created this space to share my research, my engineering projects and my thoughts on water challenges across Africa.

What you can expect here

  • Research updates on my work on low-cost adsorbents for removing PFAS and microplastics from drinking water.
  • Field notes from groundwater quality and water supply projects.
  • Practical guides on tools I use every day: QGIS, Python, Power BI and MODFLOW.
  • Reflections on studying, scholarships and growing as an engineer-researcher.

Safe water is not a luxury. It is the foundation of health, education and opportunity.

Thank you for stopping by. Feel free to reach out if you would like to collaborate.

Let's work on water together.

Open to research collaborations, PhD opportunities and consulting on water quality and supply.
