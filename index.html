About

I am a civil and water resources engineer currently pursuing an MSc in Water Engineering at the Pan African University Institute for Water and Energy Sciences (PAUWES), Algeria, on a fully funded African Union scholarship.

I graduated with First Class Honours in Civil Engineering from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria, and have worked on water supply, irrigation and environmental assessment projects across Nigeria. My research focuses on groundwater quality and on developing low-cost adsorbents for removing PFAS and microplastics from drinking water.

I am also the founder of WASH for Africa's Innovation and Development (WASHAID).

Beyond engineering, I write personal essays on Medium and Substack, and I play chess.

A Climate-Resilient Framework for Energy-Efficient Data Center Cooling
